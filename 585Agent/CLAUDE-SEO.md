## Content Workflows

### 1. Service Area Pages
Create one dedicated page per town in the service area. Each page must:

**Structure:**
- `<title>` tag: `[Service] in [Town], NY | 585 Locksmith`
- `<h1>`: `Locksmith in [Town], NY — Fast, Local, 24/7`
- Opening paragraph: 100–150 words mentioning the town name, service area, and primary service
- Services section: list all 6 services with 2–3 sentences each, mentioning [Town] at least once
- Why choose us section: family-owned, licensed, fast response
- CTA: click-to-call phone number
- Footer: business name, phone, service area

**Tone:** Helpful and local. Mention recognizable landmarks, neighborhoods, or context for that town where natural. Never sound generic.

**Do not:**
- Copy-paste the same page for every town — Google penalizes duplicate content
- Keyword-stuff. Use the town name naturally, not more than once every 100 words.

**Example towns to prioritize first:** Greece, Irondequoit, Brighton, Pittsford, Henrietta

### 3. FAQ Page
Create a single FAQ page targeting question-based searches. Format each entry with `<h3>` for the question and a paragraph for the answer. Cover:

- How much does a locksmith cost in Rochester NY?
- How fast can you respond to an emergency?

Use natural language in answers. Each answer should be 50–100 words — enough to be useful, short enough to be scannable.

## Review Management

Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor after GBP relevance. Target: **4.8+ stars, 50+ reviews within 6 months.**

### Asking for reviews
After every completed job, send the customer a follow-up text:

> "Hi [Name], this is [Tech] from 585 Locksmith — glad we could help today! If you have 30 seconds, a Google review would mean a lot to us: [Google Review Link]. Thanks!"

Send within 1 hour of job completion while the experience is fresh.

### Review response templates

**5-star review:**
> Thank you so much, [Name]! We're really glad we could get you taken care of quickly. If you ever need us again, don't hesitate to call — we're here 24/7. — The 585 Locksmith Team

**5-star review mentioning a specific service:**
> Thank you, [Name]! [Car lockouts / House lockouts / Rekeying] can be really stressful, and we're glad we could make it easy. We appreciate your trust — and your kind words. — 585 Locksmith, Rochester NY

**4-star review:**
> Thanks so much for the kind words, [Name]! We always want to earn that fifth star — if there's anything we could have done better, feel free to reach out directly at (585) 454-9740. We appreciate your business.

**Negative review (1–2 stars):**
> Hi [Name], thank you for letting us know. We take every experience seriously and want to make this right. Please call or text us directly at (585) 454-9740 so we can address this personally. — 585 Locksmith

Never argue with a negative review publicly. The response is for every future customer reading it — not for the reviewer.

## What You Never Do

- Never keyword-stuff a page title, heading, or body copy
- Never create duplicate content across area pages — every page must be meaningfully different
- Never buy reviews or post fake reviews — this violates Google policy and will get the listing suspended
- Never make claims that can't be verified ("best locksmith in Rochester") without a citation
- Never ignore a negative review — always respond within 24 hours
- Never report on metrics that don't tie back to calls, leads, or rankings — no vanity stats
